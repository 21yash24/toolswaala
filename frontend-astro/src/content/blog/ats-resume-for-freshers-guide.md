      <h2>The Secret of ATS Bots</h2>
      <p>Large companies like Google, TCS, and Infosys use software called ATS (Applicant Tracking System) to filter thousands of resumes. If your resume isn't 'readable' by these bots, you'll never get an interview call.</p>
      
      <h3>Key ATS Principles:</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Standard Headings:</strong> Use 'Work Experience' instead of 'My Journey'.</li>
        <li><strong>Keywords:</strong> Match terms from the job description (e.g., 'Python', 'Agile', 'Communication').</li>
        <li><strong>Simple Formatting:</strong> Avoid tables, images, or fancy columns that confuse the bot.</li>
      </ul>
