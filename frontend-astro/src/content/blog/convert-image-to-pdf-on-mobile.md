      <h2>The Best Way to Share Documents</h2>
      <p>Sending 10 separate JPG images is annoying for the receiver. A single PDF is easier to read, search, and print. It's the professional way to share school notes or bills.</p>
      
      <h3>Steps to Convert:</h3>
      <ol>
        <li>Click 'Upload Images'.</li>
        <li>Select all the photos from your gallery.</li>
        <li>Drag to reorder them if needed.</li>
        <li>Click 'Generate PDF' and download.</li>
      </ol>
