      <h2>The Dreaded 75% Rule</h2>
      <p>In most Indian colleges and universities (UGC regulated), 75% attendance is mandatory to appear for semester exams. Falling below this can lead to being 'detained' or 'debarred'.</p>
      
      <h3>The Math of Bunking</h3>
      <p>To stay above 75%, you need to ensure that for every 4 classes scheduled, you attend at least 3. If you've attended 30 out of 40 classes, you are exactly at 75%.</p>

      <h2>How to Calculate Your Status</h2>
      <p>Formula to check if you can bunk more classes:</p>
      <p><strong>Safe Bunks = (Present / 0.75) - Total Classes</strong></p>
