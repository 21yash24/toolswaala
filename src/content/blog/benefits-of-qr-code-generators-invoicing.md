Adding a dynamic UPI QR code to digital invoices allows customers to scan and pay instantly using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM.

### Key Benefits for Indian Businesses
- **Zero Payment Gateway Commissions:** Direct bank-to-bank UPI transfers have 0% MDR fees.
- **Faster Invoice Settlement:** Customers pay immediately upon receiving the PDF.
- **Error-Free Transfers:** Pre-encoded UPI VPA and bill amount prevent manual typing errors.
