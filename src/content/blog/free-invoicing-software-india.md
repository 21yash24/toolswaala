### Key Features of Indian GST Invoices
- **GSTIN Verification:** Valid 15-digit GST identification number for supplier and buyer.
- **HSN/SAC Codes:** 4 to 8 digit classification codes for goods and services.
- **Tax Split:** CGST + SGST for intra-state billing (e.g. 9% + 9%) or IGST (18%) for inter-state sales.
- **Place of Supply:** State code declaration for tax jurisdiction.
