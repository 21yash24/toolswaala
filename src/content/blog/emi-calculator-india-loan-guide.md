      <h2>The True Cost of a Loan</h2>
      <p>A personal loan at 12% for 5 years might seem cheaper than a car loan at 9% for 7 years because of the interest accumulation. Tenures change everything.</p>
      
      <h3>Comparison Table:</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Home Loan:</strong> Low interest (8-10%), Long tenure (15-30 years).</li>
        <li><strong>Personal Loan:</strong> High interest (11-18%), Short tenure (1-5 years).</li>
        <li><strong>Car Loan:</strong> Medium interest (9-12%), Medium tenure (3-7 years).</li>
      </ul>
