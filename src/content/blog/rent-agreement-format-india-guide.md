      <h2>The 11-Month Rule</h2>
      <p>Most residential rent agreements in India are for 11 months to avoid the mandatory registration requirement under the Registration Act, 1908.</p>
      
      <h3>Critical Clauses:</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Notice Period:</strong> Usually 1 month.</li>
        <li><strong>Rent Increment:</strong> Standard is 5-10% annually.</li>
        <li><strong>Security Deposit:</strong> Mention if it's refundable or carries deductions.</li>
        <li><strong>Maintenance Charges:</strong> Clarify who pays society fees.</li>
      </ul>
