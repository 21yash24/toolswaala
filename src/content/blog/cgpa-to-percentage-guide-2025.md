      <h2>The Standard Multiplier: Why 9.5?</h2>
      <p>Most Indian boards, including CBSE, use the standard multiplier of 9.5 to convert CGPA to an approximate percentage. This formula is designed to account for the fact that a perfect 10 CGPA doesn't necessarily mean 100% in raw marks.</p>
      
      <h3>How to calculate:</h3>
      <p><strong>Percentage (%) = CGPA × 9.5</strong></p>
      <p>For example, if your CGPA is 8.4, your percentage would be 8.4 × 9.5 = 79.8%.</p>

      <h2>University Specific Formulas</h2>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Mumbai University:</strong> Percentage = (CGPA * 7.1) + 11 (For Engineering)</li>
        <li><strong>AKTU:</strong> Percentage = (CGPA - 0.75) * 10</li>
        <li><strong>GTU:</strong> Percentage = (CGPA - 0.5) * 10</li>
      </ul>
