College life in India requires managing semester exams, maintaining the mandatory **75% UGC attendance rule**, converting SGPA/CGPA for job applications, and crafting ATS-friendly resumes. Here is a curated list of the top 10 best free online tools every Indian student should bookmark in 2025.

### 1. University CGPA to Percentage Calculator
Different Indian universities use distinct grading formulas. For instance, Mumbai University uses `(7.1 × CGPA) + 11`, while VTU and AKTU follow `(CGPA - 0.75) × 10`. Using a generic multiplier can lead to errors on job applications and higher study forms.

### 2. UGC 75% Attendance & Bunk Calculator
Under the UGC guidelines, students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance to sit for semester examinations. An attendance calculator helps you track present and absent days and computes the exact number of classes you can safely skip per subject.
