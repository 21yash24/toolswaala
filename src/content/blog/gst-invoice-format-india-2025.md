      <h2>Mandatory GST Invoice Fields</h2>
      <p>Under GST law, every B2B invoice must contain specific information to allow your customers to claim Input Tax Credit (ITC).</p>
      
      <h3>Key Requirements:</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>GSTIN:</strong> Both yours and the customer's (for B2B).</li>
        <li><strong>HSN Code:</strong> Mandatory for goods (4-8 digits based on turnover).</li>
        <li><strong>Place of Supply:</strong> To determine if it's IGST or CGST+SGST.</li>
        <li><strong>Invoice Numbering:</strong> Must be consecutive and unique for a financial year.</li>
      </ul>
