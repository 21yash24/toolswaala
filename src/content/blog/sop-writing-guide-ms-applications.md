      <h2>What is an SOP?</h2>
      <p>A Statement of Purpose (SOP) is an essay that outlines your academic background, career goals, and why you are choosing a specific university. It is the most critical part of your study abroad application.</p>
      
      <h3>The 5-Paragraph Structure:</h3>
      <ol>
        <li><strong>Introduction:</strong> Your hook and why you are passionate about the field.</li>
        <li><strong>Background:</strong> Your academic achievements and projects.</li>
        <li><strong>Professional Experience:</strong> Internships, jobs, or research work.</li>
        <li><strong>Why This Course/Uni:</strong> Specific professors or labs you like.</li>
        <li><strong>Conclusion:</strong> Your long-term career vision.</li>
      </ol>
