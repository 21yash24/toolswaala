Over 75% of resumes submitted for job openings in India are rejected by automated Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) before reaching HR. For college freshers, crafting a clean, scannable resume structure is critical to securing interviews.

### Step 1: Use Standard Section Headers
- **Education:** Degree, college name, graduation year, CGPA / Percentage.
- **Technical Skills:** Languages, frameworks, software proficiency.
- **Projects:** Title, technology stack, metrics, GitHub link.
- **Work / Internships:** Role, company, duration, key accomplishments.
