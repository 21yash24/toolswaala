      <p>The New Tax Regime is now the default. However, if you have high investments in LIC, HRA, and 80C, the Old Regime might still be better for you.</p>
      
      <h3>Thresholds to Consider:</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Up to ₹7 Lakh:</strong> Zero tax in New Regime (due to rebate).</li>
        <li><strong>High Deductions:</strong> If your total deductions exceed ₹3.75 Lakh, Old Regime is usually better.</li>
